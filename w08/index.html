Week 8 · Practical P8 · 2 hours

Redesign a pedestrian crossing and a cycle facility

Objective

Redesign one pedestrian crossing and one cycle facility on your corridor, addressing the principal conflicts between users (LO3).

Tasks

Task 1. Choose and document the site

Pick the crossing on your corridor that your LAB8 audit or the crossing-gap map rates worst, and the cycle segment with the lowest CEQI (or, if none was scored, the segment with the most sidewalk riding). Photograph both from safe positions and record lanes, speed limit, crossing length, signal timing (if any) and kerbside use.

Deliverable: Site sheet with photos and measured dimensions

Task 2. Map the conflicts

Draw a plan of the existing layout. Mark each conflict: turning vehicles vs pedestrians, parked cars blocking sightlines, cyclists vs boarding passengers, cyclists vs pedestrians on shared sidewalks. Rank them by severity using the LAB8 barrier codes.

Deliverable: Conflict plan with a ranked list

Task 3. Redesign the crossing

Produce a plan (1:200) of the redesigned crossing: kerb extensions, refuge island, raised table or signals, kerb ramps and tactile paving, lighting, and parking removed from the approach. State the crossing length before and after and the pedestrian green time needed for a walker at 0.9 m/s.

Deliverable: Crossing plan with before/after crossing length and timing

Task 4. Redesign the cycle facility

Choose the facility type from the speed and traffic on the street (LEC16 table) and show it in cross-section and in plan through the nearest junction and bus stop. Explain how you keep it continuous and how you treat the bus stop.

Deliverable: Cross-section and plan of the cycle facility through one junction and one stop

Task 5. Who gains, who gives up

List what each user group gains and loses (pedestrians, cyclists, bus passengers, drivers, shopkeepers). Name one measurement you would take after construction to show that the conflict was reduced.

Deliverable: Half-page justification (feeds project Stage 3, Week 10)

Materials

  • LAB8 audit results and maps
  • LAB08_barriers.gpkg
  • Tape measure, phone camera
  • LEC15–16 slides

Assessment

Continuous assessment (LO3): conflicts correctly identified and ranked, design that addresses the worst conflict, dimensions consistent with the street width, and a credible after-measurement.

Week 8 · Laboratory LAB8 · 2 hours · QGIS, Excel (QGIS 3.40 LTR or newer)

Mapping barriers and safety deficiencies in walking and cycling networks

Objective

Use the field-audited cycling network, open map data and your own georeferenced images to map barriers in walking and cycling networks and document safety deficiencies (LO2, LO3).

Data

FileDescriptionSource
LAB08_barriers.gpkgLayers: districts, cycling_segments (1,913 audited segments with CEQI and components; 843 scored), crossings (control, island, tactile_paving), main_roads, main_road_samples_50m (distance to nearest crossing), schools (signalised crossings within 300 m), bus_stops, audit_points (empty template for your photos)Normukhammadov (2026); © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
LAB08_ceqi_barriers.xlsxSheets: Segments (843 scored), CEQI_by_district (live AVERAGEIFS/COUNTIF/PERCENTILE), Lowest_links (three per district, weakest component by formula), Crossing_gaps, Schools, Audit_form (with code lookup), Barrier_codesNormukhammadov (2026); © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)

All GIS layers use EPSG:32642 (WGS 84 / UTM zone 42N). Set the QGIS project CRS to EPSG:32642 before starting.

Steps

Step 1. Set up the project

  1. Project > Properties > CRS: EPSG:32642
  2. Layer > Add Layer > Add Vector Layer: add all layers of LAB08_barriers.gpkg
  3. Style cycling_segments by ceqi (Symbology > Graduated, Quantile, 5 classes); unscored segments grey

Check: 843 segments have a CEQI value (Attribute table > filter: "ceqi" IS NOT NULL)

Step 2. Lowest-quality links per district

  1. Select by Expression: "ceqi" <= 0.319 (the 10th percentile from CEQI_by_district)
  2. Processing Toolbox > Vector analysis > Statistics by categories: input = selected segments, field = ceqi, category = district
  3. Compare with the CEQI_by_district and Lowest_links sheets; note the weakest component of each lowest link

Check: 85 segments selected; Yakkasaroy has the lowest district mean (0.464)

Step 3. Crossing gaps on main roads

  1. Open main_road_samples_50m (points every 50 m, created with Vector geometry > Points along geometry)
  2. Recompute one district: Processing Toolbox > Vector analysis > Distance to nearest hub (points), hubs = crossings
  3. Select by Expression: "dist_crossing_m" > 200; compare the share with the Crossing_gaps sheet

Check: City-wide share of main-road samples more than 200 m from a crossing = 0.311

Step 4. Schools without a signalised crossing

  1. Select crossings where control = 'signalised'; Vector > Geoprocessing > Buffer the schools by 300 m
  2. Vector > Analysis Tools > Count Points in Polygon (selected crossings only) and compare with signalised_300m
  3. Pick the three schools nearest your corridor and inspect them in a satellite basemap

Check: 287 of 486 schools have no signalised crossing within 300 m

Step 5. Field audit with georeferenced images

  1. Walk or ride 1–2 km of your corridor from safe positions and photograph each barrier with location services on
  2. Processing Toolbox > Vector creation > Import geotagged photos: input folder = your photos; save as a layer
  3. Copy the points into audit_points (Edit > Paste Features) and fill barrier_code, severity, user_group, description and proposed_fix
  4. Copy the attribute table into the Audit_form sheet; column M looks up each code's description

Check: At least 10 audit points, each with a code from Barrier_codes and a severity

Step 6. Document the deficiencies

  1. Make a map: lowest-decile CEQI links, crossing-gap samples, schools without signals and your audit points
  2. In Excel, count your audit points by code and severity (Insert > PivotTable)
  3. Write the three most serious deficiencies with location, users affected and a proposed fix

Check: Map with legend and sources; a ranked list of three deficiencies

Submission checklist

  • Barrier map of your corridor (PNG/PDF with legend, sources and date)
  • audit_points layer with at least 10 coded photos (faces and plates blurred or excluded)
  • Completed Audit_form sheet and a pivot of barriers by code and severity
  • Ranked list of the three most serious safety deficiencies with proposed fixes
  • Half a page of limitations: OSM completeness, CEQI covers only surveyed central streets, single-day audit
  • Input data, method, results and limitations are all included (syllabus §6).
