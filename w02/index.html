Week 2 · Practical P2 · 2 hours

Field-survey plan, recording form and vehicle versus person flows

Objective

Prepare a field-survey plan and recording form for your corridor and compare vehicle flows with person flows using sample calculations (LO2).

Tasks

Task 1. Link the survey to your indicators

Take your P1 indicator table. For each indicator that needs field data, state what will be observed (vehicles by class, occupancy, pedestrians, cyclists, bus headways, journey times) and the precision you need.

Deliverable: Table: indicator → observation → precision

Task 2. Plan location, periods and sampling

Choose one or two counting positions on your corridor that are safe and give a clear view. Choose periods (morning peak, inter-peak, evening peak) and at least two weekdays. Use 15-minute intervals. State what you will not observe (night, weekend, other seasons) and why.

Deliverable: Survey plan: map of positions, timetable, sampling statement

Task 3. Design the recording form

Draw a form with one row per 15-minute interval and columns for each vehicle class, plus a separate occupancy sample (for example: occupants of every tenth car, load of every bus as empty / seated / standing / full). Add header fields for date, position, direction, weather and observer. Collect no plate numbers, faces or names.

Deliverable: Recording form (one A4 page) and a filled test row

07:00–07:15
07:15–07:30

Task 4. Pilot count

Count for 15 minutes at your position (or, in class, use the cleaned LAB2 synthetic counts for one hour). Sample occupancy for at least 20 cars and every bus that passes.

Deliverable: Pilot data, entered in a spreadsheet

Task 5. Vehicle flows versus person flows

Convert your pilot to hourly flows. Compute person flows = vehicles × average occupancy by class. Compare the share of each mode in vehicles and in persons. Then repeat with the LAB2 Assumptions sheet occupancies and explain the difference.

Deliverable: Two-row table (vehicles, persons) by class, and three sentences of interpretation

Task 6. Data-quality plan

List three things that could go wrong in your survey (missed intervals, double counting at shift change, unclear classes) and how you will detect and log each one.

Deliverable: Data-quality plan, to be used with the LAB2 DQ_log template

Materials

  • P1 indicator table
  • LAB2 counts workbook (Assumptions and Counts_clean sheets)
  • Clipboard, stopwatch or phone timer
  • Safety vest

Assessment

Continuous assessment (LO2): a survey plan that serves the indicators, a usable recording form free of personal data, and a correct vehicle-to-person conversion with stated occupancy sources.

Week 2 · Laboratory LAB2 · 2 hours · QGIS, Excel (QGIS 3.40 LTR or newer)

Cleaning survey data and plotting flows over time

Objective

Clean a real GPS survey sample and a synthetic classified count, check units, plot flows over time and prepare a data-quality log (LO2).

Data

SYNTHETIC — generated for teaching: LAB02_counts_synthetic.xlsx. These files imitate realistic Tashkent conditions but are not observations; the README sheet records the method and random seed.

FileDescriptionSource
LAB02_gps_sample.gpkgReal GPS points from Normukhammadov's field survey, 12 July 2025, 10:00–10:12 local time: gps_raw_points (6,273 rows from two folders, file order) and gps_clean_points_referenceNormukhammadov (2026)
LAB02_gps_cleaning.xlsxSheets: Summary (formulas), GPS_raw (file order, step-distance and km/h formulas), Flags (sorted by time; duplicate and range flags), Clean_track (kept rows; time steps, distances, gap rule), DQ_log (template with one example)Normukhammadov (2026)
LAB02_counts_synthetic.xlsxSYNTHETIC three-day classified count (15-minute intervals, two directions) with planted errors. Sheets: Counts_raw (as typed, with check formulas), Interval_check, Counts_clean (reference), Assumptions (occupancies), Hourly (SUMIFS), DQ_logSynthetic teaching data

All GIS layers use EPSG:32642 (WGS 84 / UTM zone 42N). Set the QGIS project CRS to EPSG:32642 before starting.

Steps

Step 1. Look at the raw GPS points

  1. Set the project CRS to EPSG:32642; Layer > Add Layer > Add Vector Layer: LAB02_gps_sample.gpkg, layer gps_raw_points
  2. Processing Toolbox > Vector creation > Points to path: order field = row_id. Repeat with order field = cts, then with t_s
  3. Style each path in a different colour and compare their shapes and lengths ($length in the Field Calculator)

Check: Ordered by cts the path jumps back and forth; ordered by t_s it follows one street

Step 2. Check units and impossible values

  1. Open LAB02_gps_cleaning.xlsx, sheet GPS_raw. Column H is speed in m/s; column L converts to km/h (=H×3.6)
  2. Data > Filter: show rows with H > 15 (54 km/h) or Fix < 2
  3. Read the maximum in the Summary sheet

Check: Maximum raw speed 403.8 km/h; 27 rows are out of range once duplicates are removed

Step 3. Remove duplicates and sort by time

  1. Sheet Flags is GPS_raw sorted by Seconds (column D) then Row id. Check that column I is TRUE when time, latitude and longitude equal the row above
  2. Data > Filter on column K = TRUE; copy the visible rows to a new sheet and compare the count with Clean_track
  3. In GPS_raw, sort by Clip time cts (Data > Sort, A→Z) and watch the step-distance total in Summary; then undo

Check: 3,123 duplicate rows; 3,123 rows kept; distance 36.93 km in file order and 59.0 km when sorted by the clip timer

Step 4. Compute the clean distance and run the validation gate

  1. In Clean_track, check the time-step formula (H), the haversine step (I) and the gap rule (J: steps over 10 s are not counted)
  2. In Summary, compare Median step with Median speed × median time step

Check: Clean distance 1.99 km; median clean speed 13.6 km/h; median step 0.4 m

Step 5. Find the errors in the count survey

  1. Open LAB02_counts_synthetic.xlsx, sheet Counts_raw. Read the README: the data are SYNTHETIC
  2. Column N counts how often each date–direction–interval appears; Data > Filter on N > 1 to see duplicates
  3. Column O compares each row with the same slot on the other days; filter O > 2.5 to find unit errors and typos
  4. Column P flags blank cells; sheet Interval_check counts missing intervals

Check: 6 duplicate rows, 8 rows recorded as hourly rates, 1 typo, 1 blank cell, 10 missing intervals

Step 6. Clean, aggregate and plot flows over time

  1. Make your own cleaned copy: delete duplicates, divide unit-error rows by 4, correct the typo from the pattern of other classes, leave the blank as blank and note it
  2. Compare your table with Counts_clean, then use the Hourly sheet (SUMIFS by date, direction and hour)
  3. Insert > Line chart: vehicles per hour by hour for each day, Eastbound; then persons per hour

Check: 15 Sep Eastbound morning peak hour 08:00 with 1,237 vehicles and 3,389 persons per hour (with the Assumptions occupancies)

Step 7. Write the data-quality log

  1. Fill the DQ_log sheet in both workbooks: one row per issue with rows affected, how detected, action taken and effect on results
  2. Mark which actions are judgements (for example the typo correction) rather than rules

Check: At least five issues logged for the counts and four for the GPS sample

  • Your own cleaned count table, the hourly vehicle and person charts, and the DQ_log
