Urban Transport Systems Planning and Design

This course is a local adaptation for Tashkent State Transport University. It draws on openly published course material on transport systems design and on urban transportation planning and policy, listed in the references. It is not an official course of any other university and implies no course equivalence. Source material is cited, never copied; figures are redrawn.

Learning outcomes

  • LO1 Define a transport problem, system boundaries and measurable outcome indicators.
  • LO2 Assess data quality and analyse demand, networks and accessibility using maps and calculations.
  • LO3 Develop alternative multimodal network, street cross-section, walking and cycling designs.
  • LO4 Quantitatively assess public transport service, junction operation and infrastructure management alternatives.
  • LO5 Analyse the social, environmental and institutional effects of transport policies and explain the distribution of benefits and costs.
  • LO6 Appraise alternatives using multiple criteria, test sensitivity to assumptions and state the limits of the evidence.
  • LO7 Prepare an implementation plan covering funding needs, responsible actors, phasing and monitoring.
  • LO8 Defend a project recommendation using maps, reproducible calculations and a concise policy brief.
