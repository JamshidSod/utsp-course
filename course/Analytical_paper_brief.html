Individual analytical paper and policy brief

The task (syllabus §4)

Prepare a 6–8-page evidence-based paper addressing one clearly defined transport decision. Compare at least two alternatives, assess their impacts on stakeholder groups and provide a recommendation suited to local conditions. Include a 1–2-page policy brief for decision-makers within the specified page limit.

Cite sources, distinguish measurements from assumptions and discuss counterarguments.

The paper is individual, even though the course project is a team project. It may use your project corridor, but it must address a different, narrower decision than the project as a whole and be written by you alone.

Required structure

  1. Decision and context (about half a page): what must be decided, by whom, by when, and why it matters now.
  2. Evidence (1–2 pages): the measurements and sources you rely on, with their date and quality; a table separating measurements from assumptions.
  3. Alternatives (1–2 pages): at least two, described so that a reader could draw them; the same criteria applied to each.
  4. Impacts by stakeholder group (1–2 pages): who gains and who loses (income, age, gender, physical ability, mode), with at least one quantified indicator.
  5. Counterarguments and limits of the evidence (about half a page): the strongest objection and your answer; what would change your recommendation.
  6. Recommendation (about half a page): suited to local conditions, with the first implementation step and who takes it.
  7. Policy brief (1–2 pages, inside the page limit): headline recommendation, three reasons, cost and risk, what to monitor; written for a khokimiyat official with no technical background.
  8. References: every source cited in the text; data sources with date of access.

Format

  • A4, 11–12 pt, single spacing; figures and tables count towards the page limit; references do not.
  • Every figure and table has a source line; every number is traceable to a source or to your own calculation file (submit the file).
  • Generative AI tools may be used only for language editing, and you must say so in a footnote; analysis, numbers and sources are your own.

Marking

The paper is marked on the five-point scale with the analytical-paper rubric in Marking_guides.docx. A paper outside the 6–8-page limit, without at least two alternatives, or without a policy brief is returned for correction before it is marked.
